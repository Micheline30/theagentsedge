How to Onboard a New Agent
Step by step setup guide
Step 1 — Buy Twilio Number
Log into your Twilio account → Phone Numbers → Buy a Number. Cost is ~$1/month per number. Assign it to the Evertrust Dialer TwiML app.
Step 2 — Create Agent Identity
Choose a unique identity string for the agent (e.g. jsmith_agent). This registers them in Twilio for inbound call routing.
Step 3 — Add Agent Here
Fill in the Add Agent form above with their name, username, PIN, Twilio number, identity, and token server URL. Click Add Agent.
Step 4 — Share Credentials
Send the agent their username and PIN. Direct them to theagentsedge.com to log in. That's it — they're ready to dial.
